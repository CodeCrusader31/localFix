SECTION 6 — PLUMBING (General Knowledge)
================================================================

Plumbing services cover the installation, repair, and maintenance of water supply lines, drainage systems, and sanitation networks. Certified plumbers handle tasks such as pipe fitting, faucet installation, water heater repair, and sewer line inspection. Proper plumbing ensures clean water access and efficient waste removal in residential and commercial spaces.

---

Common plumbing problems include leaking faucets, burst pipes, clogged drains, low water pressure, and running toilets. A leaking faucet can waste thousands of liters of water annually and is often caused by a worn washer or O-ring. Burst pipes are typically caused by freezing temperatures, corrosion, or excessive pressure and require immediate professional attention.

---

Clogged drains are among the most frequent plumbing issues and are usually caused by the buildup of hair, grease, soap scum, or foreign objects. Plumbers use drain snakes, hydro-jetting, and chemical treatments to clear blockages. Preventive measures include installing drain strainers and avoiding pouring grease or food waste down the sink.

---

Low water pressure in a home can result from mineral deposits in pipes, a failing pressure regulator, or leaks in the supply line. A licensed plumber will diagnose the root cause using pressure gauges and pipe inspection tools. Replacing aging galvanized pipes with copper or PEX tubing can significantly improve water flow.

---

Water heater issues are common plumbing service requests and include insufficient hot water, strange noises, or complete heater failure. Sediment buildup inside the tank reduces heating efficiency and may cause rumbling sounds during operation. Flushing the water heater tank annually and checking the anode rod extends the unit's lifespan considerably.

---

Septic tanks in homes not connected to municipal sewage systems need to be pumped every three to five years depending on household size and usage. Flushing non-biodegradable items like wipes, cotton swabs, or sanitary products can clog and damage the septic system. Avoid using harsh chemical drain cleaners that kill beneficial bacteria necessary for septic system function.

---

Plumbing pipes in older homes should be inspected for corrosion, mineral buildup, and joint integrity every few years. Galvanized steel pipes are particularly prone to rust and should be replaced with copper or PEX alternatives. Early detection of pipe degradation prevents expensive water damage and mold growth inside walls.

---

Plumbing costs vary based on the complexity of the issue, the type of pipes or fixtures involved, and whether excavation or wall cutting is required. Emergency or after-hours plumbing services usually carry a higher call-out charge. Getting at least two quotes for major plumbing repairs helps ensure fair pricing.

SECTION 7 — ELECTRICAL SERVICES (General Knowledge)
================================================================

Electrical services involve the installation, inspection, and repair of wiring systems, circuit breakers, outlets, switches, and lighting. Licensed electricians ensure that all work complies with local safety codes and national electrical standards. Faulty electrical systems are a leading cause of house fires, making professional intervention critical.

---

Common electrical problems in homes include tripping circuit breakers, flickering lights, non-functional outlets, and overloaded circuits. A circuit breaker trips as a safety mechanism when a circuit draws more current than it is rated for. Persistent tripping often indicates wiring issues, faulty appliances, or the need for a panel upgrade.

---

Flickering lights can be caused by loose wiring connections, voltage fluctuations, or incompatible dimmer switches. If flickering is isolated to one fixture, the issue is likely a loose bulb or faulty switch. Widespread flickering across multiple rooms may indicate a problem with the main electrical panel or the utility connection.

---

Electrical short circuits occur when a live wire touches a neutral wire, causing a sudden surge of current. Signs of short circuits include burnt smells, scorch marks on outlets, or fuses that blow repeatedly. Electricians resolve short circuits by identifying the fault location, repairing or replacing damaged wiring, and testing all affected circuits.

---

Outdoor and garden electrical work includes the installation of security lighting, power outlets, and wiring for garden equipment. All outdoor electrical components must be rated for exterior use and protected by ground fault circuit interrupters (GFCIs). Proper waterproofing and conduit installation prevent moisture damage and electric shock hazards.

---

Home wiring should be inspected by a licensed electrician every five to ten years, especially in older properties. Signs that an electrical system needs attention include frequently tripping breakers, discolored outlets, a burning smell near sockets, or lights that dim when appliances are turned on. Outdated wiring types like aluminum or knob-and-tube wiring pose fire risks and should be replaced.

---

Surge protectors and voltage stabilizers protect sensitive home electronics from power surges caused by lightning strikes, utility switching, or large appliances cycling on and off. Installing a whole-home surge protector at the main electrical panel provides broader protection than individual outlet-level surge strips. Replacing surge protectors every two to three years ensures continued effectiveness.

SECTION 8 — AC REPAIR (General Knowledge)
================================================================

Air conditioning (AC) repair services address issues with cooling performance, airflow, refrigerant levels, and system noise. Technicians are trained to work with split systems, window units, central air systems, and inverter ACs. Regular AC servicing is essential to maintain energy efficiency and extend the equipment's operational life.

---

One of the most common AC problems is insufficient cooling, which can result from low refrigerant levels, a dirty air filter, or a malfunctioning compressor. Low refrigerant is typically caused by a leak in the system and must be identified and repaired before recharging the coolant. Running an AC with low refrigerant can permanently damage the compressor.

---

AC units that produce unusual noises such as grinding, banging, or squealing often have mechanical issues like a loose fan blade, failing motor bearings, or debris inside the unit. Rattling sounds may indicate loose screws or panels. Technicians inspect internal components to isolate the source of noise and carry out targeted repairs.

---

Water leaking from an indoor AC unit is usually caused by a blocked condensate drain line, a frozen evaporator coil, or a damaged drain pan. Blocked drain lines allow water to overflow into the unit, potentially causing mold growth and water damage. Cleaning the drain line every few months with a diluted bleach solution prevents blockages.

---

AC units that frequently turn on and off in short cycles, a condition known as short cycling, may have an oversized unit relative to the room, a refrigerant issue, or a malfunctioning thermostat. Short cycling increases energy consumption and reduces the lifespan of compressor components. A technician will assess the system sizing and refrigerant charge to resolve the issue.

---

AC units should be serviced at least once a year, ideally before the summer season when usage peaks. Regular servicing includes cleaning the air filters, checking refrigerant levels, inspecting electrical connections, and lubricating moving parts. Neglected AC units consume more electricity and are more prone to sudden breakdowns.

---

Air filters in AC systems should be cleaned or replaced every one to three months depending on usage and the level of dust in the environment. A clogged air filter reduces airflow, causing the system to work harder and leading to higher electricity bills. Most modern AC units have washable filters that can be rinsed with water and allowed to dry before reinsertion.

---

The cost of AC servicing typically depends on the type of unit, the number of indoor units, and whether parts replacement is required. Basic servicing involving cleaning and gas pressure checking is generally less expensive than a full repair involving compressor or PCB replacement. Always request an itemized quote before authorizing any repair work.

SECTION 9 — CARPENTRY (General Knowledge)
================================================================

Carpentry services include the fabrication, installation, and repair of wooden structures such as furniture, cabinets, doors, windows, flooring, and wall paneling. Skilled carpenters work with different types of wood, plywood, MDF, and composite materials to deliver custom or standard solutions. Good carpentry enhances both the functionality and aesthetics of a living space.

---

Common carpentry requests include fixing squeaky floors, repairing broken furniture joints, installing shelving units, and fitting custom wardrobes. Squeaky floors are often caused by wood rubbing against nails or subflooring gaps due to changes in humidity. Carpenters address this by securing the floorboards with screws or adding lubrication between boards.

---

Door and window frame repairs are frequent carpentry jobs, especially in older homes where wood warps or swells with moisture. A misaligned door can be caused by settling of the house foundation or frame distortion. Carpenters shave, refit, or replace frames and hinges to restore smooth operation.

---

Custom furniture and cabinetry services allow homeowners to maximize space with built-in solutions like kitchen cabinets, bathroom vanities, and bedroom wardrobes. Carpenters take measurements, recommend materials, and fabricate pieces that match existing decor. Built-in furniture is often more space-efficient than freestanding options.

---

Wooden furniture and fixtures in humid environments are prone to warping, swelling, and mold growth. Applying wood sealant or varnish periodically and keeping indoor humidity levels between 40 and 60 percent helps preserve wooden elements. Carpenters recommend inspecting wooden doors, windows, and floor joints annually for signs of moisture damage.

---

Pest infestations can damage wooden structures through termite activity. It is important to inspect wooden beams, floors, and furniture for hollow sounds or fine sawdust-like droppings. Termite treatment and preventive wood treatments should be coordinated with pest control professionals, and using termite-resistant lumber during construction reduces long-term risk.

SECTION 14 — HOME MAINTENANCE TIPS
================================================================

Good home maintenance prevents small issues from becoming expensive repairs. Keeping a maintenance calendar that tracks service dates for AC, geysers, electrical inspections, and plumbing checks ensures timely attention. Many home services platforms offer annual maintenance contracts that bundle multiple services at a discounted rate.

---

Roof and wall waterproofing is an essential maintenance task that prevents seepage, dampness, and structural damage during the monsoon or rainy season. Waterproof coatings, sealants, and drainage systems must be checked and maintained annually. Signs of water seepage include peeling paint, damp patches on walls, and musty odors indoors.

---

Geysers and water heaters should be serviced annually to check the heating element, thermostat, pressure relief valve, and internal corrosion. Descaling the heating element removes mineral deposits that reduce heating efficiency. The pressure relief valve should be tested regularly to ensure it releases safely when pressure exceeds safe limits.

---

Kitchen chimneys and range hoods accumulate grease in their filters and should be cleaned every one to three months depending on cooking frequency. Mesh filters can be soaked in hot water with a degreasing solution, while baffle filters can often be cleaned in a dishwasher. A poorly maintained chimney filter reduces suction power and poses a fire risk from grease ignition.

---

Smart home devices such as water leak sensors, electrical load monitors, and thermostat controllers can alert homeowners to problems early. Leak sensors placed near water heaters, washing machines, and under sinks detect moisture and send alerts before damage occurs. Integrating these devices with a home services platform allows for faster professional response when issues are detected.
